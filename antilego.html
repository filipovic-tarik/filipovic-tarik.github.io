Prediction-market consistency engine

Find the prices that disagree

Antilego is a local research application that discovers related prediction-market contracts, identifies the mathematical rules connecting them, and monitors executable prices for contradictions with those rules.

02 / The problem

A market can look normal while related prices contradict one another.

A single contract price may appear reasonable in isolation. The inconsistency becomes visible only when it is compared with contracts that describe an earlier deadline, a harder threshold, an exhaustive outcome set, or a logical implication.

Antilego treats those relationships as mathematical constraints and tests the prices as a connected family rather than as unrelated bets.

04 / Probability laws

The model proposes relationships. Code checks the math.

AI is used to organize contracts and select a supported relationship. Every actual consistency decision is then made by deterministic code.

01

Deadline nesting

An earlier cumulative deadline cannot be more likely than a later one.

02

Survival deadlines

Continuing through a later date cannot be more likely than continuing through an earlier one.

03

Threshold chains

A harder ordered threshold cannot be more likely than an easier threshold.

04

Exhaustive outcomes

Mutually exclusive outcomes that cover every result should total 100 percent.

05

Complements

A proposition and its exact opposite should total 100 percent.

06

Non-exhaustive exclusivity

Outcomes that cannot co-occur may not exceed 100 percent when combined.

07

Logical implication

If one event implies another, it cannot carry the higher probability.

05 / Executable evidence

A theoretical discrepancy is not enough.

Antilego reads public order books and evaluates the prices available for an executable share quantity. Empty or insufficient books are excluded instead of being presented as opportunities.

Experimental research application

A violation is a lead, not a guaranteed trade

Fees, liquidity, resolution rules, and changing order books still require verification before any capital is placed. Antilego is designed to narrow the search and make the reasoning inspectable.
