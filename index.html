Machine learning · Handwriting recognition

Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

A handwritten OCR project that recognizes digits, individual uppercase and lowercase letters and words. The pure character reader reaches 86.49% character accuracy; adding a 50,000-word English context dictionary raises performance to 93.90% for context-assisted word accuracy for separated handwritten characters.

  • Python
  • K-nearest neighbors
  • EMNIST

Mathematical modeling · Independent paper

Modelling Earth’s Trajectory Through the Universe

Building off of a 22-page paper I wrote during my senior year of high school, this project uses calculus and orbital mechanics to build a scalable three-dimensional parametric model of Earth’s trajectory through the universe relative to Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way.

  • Parametric modeling
  • Orbital mechanics
  • GeoGebra 3D

A local market-research application that filters and groups active related Polymarket contracts, determines which probability laws should govern their relationships, and continuously monitors executable order-book prices for inconsistencies.
