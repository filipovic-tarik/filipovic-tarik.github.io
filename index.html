Earth’s Trajectory Through the Universe

Building on a 22-page paper I wrote during my senior year of high school, this project uses calculus and orbital mechanics to build a scalable three-dimensional model of Earth’s trajectory through the universe relative to Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way.

  • Parametric modeling
  • Orbital mechanics
  • GeoGebra 3D
