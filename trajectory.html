The ecliptic and Galactic planes

The amber Galactic plane is the imaginary plane enclosed by the Sun’s orbit around the Milky Way. The blue ecliptic plane is enclosed by Earth’s orbit around the Sun. They intersect at 60.3°—not at the perpendicular 90° angle that might seem intuitive. The model therefore tilts the initially upright Earth-orbit loop by the complementary 29.7°.
